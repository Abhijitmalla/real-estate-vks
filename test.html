About Brand

With the blessings of Sri Aurobindo and The Mother, Indian Silk House Agencies began its journey in 1930. It continues to guide us every day to make the beauty of Indian sarees accessible to every woman while honouring the country's rich weaving traditions.

Our story began with the vision and determination of Sri Suresh Chandra Kandoi, who started our first store in 1930. His sons, Sri Dilip Kumar Samaddar and Sri Tinkari Kumar Samaddar, carried this legacy forward by establishing more retail outlets.

Who we are

Indian Silk House Agencies - A legacy of Bengal's Heritage Silk Sarees

Established as a platform dedicated to traditional silk sarees, Indian Silk House Agencies has grown to a trusted name offering a diverse range of sarees from across India. With over 15,000+ exquisite designs and more than 3,00,000 happy customers, we continue to uphold our promise of authenticity, quality and unmatched variety.

We are committed to taking the timeless beauty of new Indian Silk Sarees to our collection, ensuring you always have access to the latest in seamless tradition and contemporary elegance. Backed by our 90+ year old brick-and-mortar legacy, we are honored to have earned the trust of many.
