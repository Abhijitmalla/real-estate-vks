Online Property Tax in Andhra Pradesh: How to Pay Swarna Panchayat House Tax

BilaBadi Editorial Team
24 Oct 2026
5 min read
2.4k Views

Key Takeaway at a Glance

Paying house tax in Andhra Pradesh's Gram Panchayats has been made hassle-free through the Swarna Panchayat online citizen portal. Property owners across all AP districts can now search by Assessment Number or Door Number, verify pending dues, and make direct online payments with instant digital receipt downloads on BilaBadi.

Paying house tax is an important civic and legal responsibility for every property owner in Andhra Pradesh. Whether you own a residential villa, an agricultural plot dwelling, a commercial storefront, or an ancestral home in a rural Panchayat, staying updated with your property tax payments ensures clear property ownership records and avoids hefty financial penalties.

In Andhra Pradesh, the Panchayat Raj and Rural Development Department introduced the Swarna Panchayat digital citizen services initiative. This modern portal simplifies tax assessment, bill generation, and online payment for rural households without requiring in-person visits to the local Gram Panchayat office.

What is Swarna Panchayat House Tax?

Swarna Panchayat House Tax is a local property levy calculated by the Gram Panchayat on buildings and residential land within its jurisdiction in Andhra Pradesh. The revenue generated is utilized for essential rural infrastructure, including street lighting, local sanitation, clean drinking water supply, and road maintenance.

Essential Details Required Before Paying Online

Before accessing the payment gateway, keep the following credentials ready to avoid session timeouts:

  • Assessment Number: The unique 10-digit property assessment code printed on your previous tax receipt or demand notice.
  • District, Mandal & Panchayat: The exact local jurisdiction under which the property is registered.
  • Door Number: The municipal or gram panchayat allotted door number.
  • Owner Name & Mobile Number: Registered mobile number for receiving transaction confirmations and OTP verification.

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Pay Swarna Panchayat Tax Online

Follow these verified steps to complete your house tax payment in under five minutes:

1

Visit the Official Citizen Portal

Go to the official Andhra Pradesh Panchayat Raj citizen services website or mobile app and navigate to the "Citizen Services - House Tax Payment" section.

2

Select Your Property Location

Choose your District (e.g., Guntur, Krishna, Visakhapatnam, Chittoor), followed by your Mandal and corresponding Gram Panchayat from the dropdown menu.

3

Search by Assessment or Door Number

Enter your unique Assessment Number. If you don't remember the number, use the alternative search to find your property by Door Number or Owner Name.

4

Verify Dues & Ownership Details

Carefully check the property owner name, building category, plinth area, current year demand, and any previous arrears listed on screen.

5

Make Payment via Digital Gateway

Select your preferred payment mode (UPI via Google Pay/PhonePe/Paytm, Net Banking, or Credit/Debit Card) and complete the secure transaction.

6

Download and Store Tax Receipt

Immediately download the digitally signed payment receipt (PDF). This receipt serves as official proof of payment for banking loans, property resale, and legal clearances on BilaBadi.

Property Tax Calculation Slabs in Andhra Pradesh

Property tax rates in Andhra Pradesh Gram Panchayats are primarily determined by the Annual Rental Value (ARV) of the property, structural type (RCC, tiled roof, thatched roof), and whether the property is self-occupied or rented out.

Property Category Structural Type Tax Rate Range Early Payment Rebate
Residential (Self-Occupied) Pucca (RCC Slab) 15% - 20% of ARV 5% Rebate if paid before April 30
Residential (Rented) Pucca / Semi-Pucca 25% - 30% of ARV 5% Rebate if paid before April 30
Commercial & Shops RCC / Framed Structure 30% - 35% of ARV 5% Rebate if paid before April 30
Vacant / Open Plot Non-Agricultural Land 0.20% - 0.50% of Market Value Nil
